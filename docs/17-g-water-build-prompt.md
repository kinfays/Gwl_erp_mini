Build Phase 1 of the G-Water module in this Laravel ERP, following `docs/17-module-g-water.md` exactly. Read that doc, `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/14-module-regional-blog.md` (region scoping pattern) and `docs/health-safety-module-design.md` §8.9–8.11 (immutable stock ledger pattern) first.

**Prerequisite (done):** Transport region scoping, see `docs/18-transport-region-scoping.md`. Use `TransportScope::vehicles($user)` for the vehicle picker, `findVehicle()` for a chosen id (404 outside reach), and `VehicleExpense::visibleTo($user)` / `MileageLog::visibleTo($user)` for the fuel and mileage links; never `Vehicle::find()` from a client value, and never re-derive the rule.

Scope of Phase 1:
- Feature flag `GWL_GWATER_MODULE_ENABLED` in `config/gwl.php` (true in `phpunit.xml`), checked in `web.php`, the sidebar via `ErpNavigation`, and Livewire `mount()`.
- Migrations (guarded with `hasTable`/`hasColumn`) for `gw_products`, `gw_prices`, `gw_purchases`, `gw_purchase_lines`, `gw_sales`, `gw_stock_movements`, `gw_opening_balances`, plus `gw_damages`, `gw_expenses`, `gw_supplier_payments` tables created now but screens come in Phase 2. Seed the module access row and the permissions `gwater.view`, `gwater.record`, `gwater.manage_prices`, `gwater.view_reports`, `gwater.void`, `gwater.export` inside a migration. Seed products: bottle (15 per pack) and sachet (30 per pack); selling prices 25 (bottle) and 10 (sachet) and purchase costs 20 (bottle) and 8 (sachet), effective 2026-01-01, plus a bottle selling price of 26 (cost still 20) effective 2026-05-12, in one dated `gw_prices` table where selling and cost can change independently; each sale/purchase line freezes the price in force on its date.
- Money in integer pesewas, quantities in integer packs. No hard deletes; void with a reason.
- Role: create role `g_water` (display name "G-Water", regional, `ict_assignable = true`, not protected) in a migration with module access and all `gwater.*` permissions (add `gwater.reimburse`); make sure `RoleGrantPolicy` treats it as a normal regional role.
- Transport link (read-only, section 5 of the doc): purchases can reference a Transport `vehicle_id`, driver, `MileageLog` and fuel `VehicleExpense` (unique per trip, never counted twice); G-Water never writes to Transport tables. Add reimbursement tracking (pending/reimbursed, payer, reference; recorder cannot reimburse their own) and a Reimbursements screen. The vehicle picker and every vehicle/driver/fuel/mileage reference go through `TransportScope` as above; refuse a trip whose vehicle is outside the actor's location. Only fuel is reimbursable; loading and offloading are paid in cash and have no reimbursement.
- Services under `app/Services/GWater/`: `GwaterVisibility` (own Head Office-or-region location only, never region alone, `super_admin` all, other location = 404), `GwaterPricingService`, `GwaterStockService` (immutable signed ledger, `lockForUpdate`, balance never negative, corrections as `adjustment`), `GwaterWorkflowService` (every write re-reads `lockForUpdate`, authorises on that copy, audits via `AuditLog::record`, voids with reversing ledger rows).
- Livewire screens: Overview (stock and month sales), Daily sales, Purchases with fuel/loading/offloading entered on the same form, Stock ledger, Prices, Opening balances. Livewire scoping trait only wraps `GwaterVisibility`; never filter by region in Blade.
- Tests (plain PHPUnit, inline fixtures): price effective dating, stock never negative, void reverses stock, location isolation (including Head Office vs a region), bottle price 25 on 11 May and 26 on 12 May 2026, opening balance posts once, audit rows written.

Do not build P&L, exports or charts yet (Phase 3). Run `php artisan test --filter=GWater` and the full suite before finishing, and update `CLAUDE.md` with a short G-Water section listing the easy-to-get-wrong rules.
