# GWL Staff Portal – End-User Manuals

These manuals explain how to use every module of the GWL Staff Portal. They are written for staff, not developers: plain language, numbered steps, and the labels exactly as they appear on screen. Each manual follows the same twelve sections (see [00-conventions.md](00-conventions.md)).

> **Draft status.** The manuals were written from the program itself (menus, screens, rules and default role set-up), not from a live demonstration. Items marked **To confirm** at the end of each manual are things that looked unfinished, ambiguous or different from the older design notes. Roles can be changed per person by the administrators, so what you see may differ from the default described here. Screenshot placeholders are written as `[Screenshot: <screen> – <what to capture>]` and still need real pictures.

Notes on the matrix:

- **Leave** is open to everyone and shown for every role.
- **Blog** is not a tab: it opens from the **Blog** link at the right of the header.
- **Global Admin** opens the **Staff Management** tab for the HR tools only (HR Dashboard, HR Analytics, Compulsory Leave, HR Contacts, Letter Settings, Acting Assignments), not the staff list. Its Assets tab is for MDM only.
- **Head Office HR** and **Regional HR** have the Letters and Visitors modules, but their default permissions give them no right to hold letters and the Visitors pages are restricted to the Receptionist role: see manuals 05 and 06.
- **Visitors** pages open only for the Receptionist role (and Super Admin); the **Kiosk** needs no login.
- **Transport** pages open for the Transport Manager, Driver and Employee roles, and Super Admin.
- **Assets** pages open for the ICT Team and Super Admin; Global Admin has MDM only.
- Roles other than the ones in the table can be created in Access Control.
- Super Admin accounts are hidden from everyone else.

## Common rules you will meet in every manual

- **Location decides reach.** Head Office is a district of its own. Staff at Head Office, in a region, or in a district see different things. A record outside your reach is shown as **not found**.
- **Nothing important is deleted.** Records are cancelled, voided or deactivated with a reason, so there is always a trail.
- **Separation of duties.** The person who prepares something does not approve it (for example: nobody approves their own leave; the sender of a payment voucher cannot review it).
- **Money** is in Ghana cedis (GH¢) with two decimals. **Dates** are shown day, month, year.
- **Files you upload** (pictures, PDFs, spreadsheets) are checked by their content and kept privately; they are never opened to the public.
