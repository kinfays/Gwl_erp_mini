## Phase 2: damages, expenses, supplier payments, owed balance
- **Damages** screen and workflow: product, packs, stage (`transit`/`loading`/`offloading`/`storage`), reason, optional trip. Posts a `damage` stock-ledger row (balance never negative), freezes the unit cost in force on its date, void reverses it.
- **Expenses** screen: categories `fuel`, `loading`, `offloading`, `other`; optional link to a trip; receipt reference; reimbursement fields on fuel rows only (`not_applicable` otherwise). **Reimbursements** screen: pending per person, mark reimbursed with a reference and date, totals; for fuel linked to Transport the amount is read from the `VehicleExpense`, with the reimbursement state held on the trip.
- **Payments to G Water**: payment requests and payments (`requested` then `paid`, or voided), reference, dates. **Owed balance** = opening owed + purchases at their frozen cost − payments paid, shown per location with a per-trip breakdown. Also the **waybill-outstanding view** from the Excel sheets: packs paid for or ordered vs packs delivered on waybills, per product, from purchases and payment requests (a report, not a second stock).
- Sidebar entries and permissions: `gwater.record` for entries, `gwater.void`, `gwater.reimburse`, `gwater.view` to read.

## Phase 3: profit and loss, overview, charts, exports
- **`GwaterProfitService`** is the ONLY P&L evaluator; screens, charts and exports all call it (like `PpeComplianceService`). Period and location in, one payload of arrays and scalars out (cacheable, keyed by the user's scope). Lines: Revenue = Σ sales amount; Cost of water sold = Σ packs sold × rolling weighted-average unit cost per product; Gross profit; Damages (packs × cost, written off at cost); Fuel; Loading and offloading; Other expenses; **Net profit** = gross − damages − fuel − labour − other. Extra measures: landed cost per pack per trip = (purchase cost + trip fuel + trip labour) ÷ packs received; margin %; break-even packs; damage rate (damaged ÷ received); trip cost per pack and cost per km where a mileage log is linked; owed to G Water. Ratios are recomputed from sums and are `null` on a zero denominator. The current period is labelled "in progress". Fuel that comes from a linked Transport expense follows that expense if it later changes, with a "changed in Transport" note on the trip. No price is ever read from the price list for history: use the frozen line prices.
- Screens: **Profit and loss** (month, quarter, custom range; per product and combined; trip-cost table; filters), and the full **Overview** (stock per product, month sales, net profit, owed to G Water, low-stock and damage-rate flags). Charts use Chart.js through its own Vite entry exactly as the other modules do (`@assets @vite('resources/js/charts.js') @endassets`, draw from `DOMContentLoaded`); no CDN script, no other chart library.
- **Exports** (permission `gwater.export` plus `gwater.view_reports`): Excel and PDF of the P&L, stock ledger, damages, expenses, reimbursements and payments. Go through one export service built on the P&L/ledger data classes (same scoped queries as the screens); Excel via Maatwebsite with a string value binder so cells starting `= + - @` stay text; PDF via Dompdf used directly; a row cap in `config/gwl.php`; every export audited (who, report, period, row count, never personal data).
- Tests: P&L arithmetic against a hand-worked month (include a price change mid-month, a damage, a fuel from Transport and a cash labour cost); weighted-average cost across two purchases at different costs; zero-denominator ratios are `null`; mid-month price change does not rewrite earlier lines; exports match the screen totals; export refused without the permission; location isolation (a Head Office user never gets a region's figures and the reverse); string cells stay text.

## Phase 4: reminders, settings, and import of past ledger lines
- **Settings** per location (`gw_settings`, kept small, overrides of `config/gwl.php` in the same way as Commercial settings): low-stock threshold in packs per product, days before an unpaid payment request is "overdue", reminder repeat interval. A settings screen needs `gwater.manage_prices`.
- **Reminders**: a scheduled command (registered in `routes/console.php` only while `GWL_GWATER_MODULE_ENABLED` is on; needs the scheduler running) that tells the G-Water users of a location, in-app through the existing general notification class, when stock is below its threshold or a payment request is overdue, at most once per repeat interval per item. A notice never carries more than product, location and counts.
- **Import of past ledger lines** (create-only, preview then confirm, re-reads the file on confirm, never overwrites, never creates a location): an .xlsx template the user can download with sheets for opening balances, purchases (waybill, date, packs per product, unit cost), daily sales (date, product, packs; price taken from the price list, a missing price blocks that row), damages, expenses and payments to G Water. Every row is posted through `GwaterWorkflowService` so stock rules, waybill uniqueness, price freezing and audit all apply; rows that fail are listed with the reason and nothing partial is committed unless the user confirms "import valid rows only". Do not read these files with the heavy PhpSpreadsheet loader if they exceed a few thousand rows; follow the import service you copied. Large amounts of historical data are expected to be small (hundreds of rows), so favour correctness over speed. Tests build tiny synthetic workbooks; never commit real files.
